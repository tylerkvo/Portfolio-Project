# Purpose

This document defines the long-term vision for tylerkvo.com and serves as the guiding principle for product and engineering decisions throughout the project's lifecycle.

While implementation details, technologies, and features will evolve over time, the vision described in this document should remain relatively stable and provide direction for future development.

---

# Vision Statement

Tylerkvo.com is (will hopefully be) more than a personal portfolio.

It is a long-term software engineering project that showcases my professional experience while serving as a hands-on environment for learning modern software architecture, product management, and engineering best practices. It honestly might never be 'complete'.

The objective is to design, build, and continuously improve a web application that reflects both who I am today and the engineer I aim to become.

---

# Product Goals

The website should:

- Showcase my professional experience in a memorable and engaging way.
- Demonstrate my ability to design, build, and maintain software products.
- Serve as a living portfolio that grows alongside my career.
- Provide interactive experiences that encourage visitors to explore the site.
- Act as a structured learning platform for modern software engineering concepts.
- Be enjoyable to maintain, iterate on, and expand over time.

---

# Success Metrics

The project will be considered successful if it:

- Accurately represents my professional experience and accomplishments.
- Demonstrates thoughtful software architecture and engineering practices.
- Is easy to maintain and extend with new features.
- Provides a polished user experience across devices.
- Encourages visitors to explore multiple areas of the website.
- Continues evolving as I learn new technologies and engineering concepts.

---

# Product Principles

Every feature added to tylerkvo.com should align with the following principles.

## Build with Purpose

Every feature should solve a meaningful problem or tell part of my story.

---

## Learn Through Building

Whenever possible, new features should introduce an opportunity to learn a new engineering concept or technology.

---

## Prioritize Maintainability

The project should favor clean architecture, documentation, and scalability over quick implementations.

---

## Deliver Incrementally

The website should improve through many small releases rather than infrequent large redesigns.

---

## Create an engaging user experience

Visitors should discover interactions, animations, and experiences that make the website memorable without sacrificing usability.
